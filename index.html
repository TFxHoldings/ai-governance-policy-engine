DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Governance decisions should be inspectable.

Deterministic

The same inputs produce the same result. Policy rules are explicit and versionable.

Explainable

Every score change and control requirement is tied to a visible rule.

Portable

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Clone it, host it, fork it, or embed the engine elsewhere.

Framework-aware

Outputs include screening signals aligned to common AI governance concerns, without pretending to replace formal legal or standards assessment.

Important: This project is an educational and portfolio demonstrator. It is not legal advice, certification, conformity assessment, or an automated determination of obligations under the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF, or any other framework.
